## SUBJECTIVE

Ms. Okonkwo is a 31-year-old female with a history of obesity (BMI 37 kg/m²), polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS), and prediabetes who presents today for follow-up and discussion of advanced pharmacologic management of her weight and metabolic status. She reports longstanding difficulty with weight management despite her efforts and describes persistent fatigue, irregular menstrual cycles, and increased carbohydrate cravings, which she attributes to her PCOS. She denies polyuria or polydipsia at this time.

She reports having tried metformin in the past, which was prescribed by a prior provider. She states she did not tolerate it particularly well and eventually discontinued it, though she has limited recall of the exact regimen details. She also notes she has made lifestyle modifications including dietary changes and increased physical activity, though specific structured program participation is not clearly documented in her chart. She has not been enrolled in a formal medically supervised weight loss program to our knowledge at this practice.

She expresses significant distress related to her weight, noting that it affects her self-esteem, energy levels, and her ability to manage her PCOS symptoms. She is motivated to pursue additional pharmacologic support and has been researching GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist therapy. She has no known drug allergies. She denies personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2.

Patient with established diagnosis of PCOS, experiencing ongoing menstrual irregularity and hormonal symptomatology. Obesity is known to exacerbate insulin resistance and androgen excess in PCOS. Severity of current endocrinologic derangement not formally reassessed today with updated labs.
