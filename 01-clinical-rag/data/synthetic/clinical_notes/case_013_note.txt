## PLAN

**Medication Requested:** Semaglutide 2.4 mg subcutaneous weekly (Wegovy) — initiating at 0.25 mg SC weekly per standard titration schedule, with planned uptitration per manufacturer protocol over approximately 16–20 weeks.

**Clinical Rationale:**
Ms. Kowalski carries a diagnosis of morbid obesity (BMI 41 kg/m²) with a comorbid major depressive disorder, both of which substantively contribute to her difficulty achieving and maintaining clinically meaningful weight loss through conventional means. GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy with semaglutide is indicated as an adjunct to reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity in adults with an initial BMI ≥30 kg/m². This patient meets BMI criteria with significant margin.

Patient was previously trialed on phentermine (dose and duration not confirmed in chart) with intolerance due to cardiovascular side effects, limiting utility of other available stimulant-based pharmacologic agents. Given the interplay between obesity and her depressive disorder, and the paucity of well-tolerated alternatives that have demonstrated efficacy at this BMI level, semaglutide is felt to be the most clinically appropriate next step.

**Prior Therapies Attempted:**

- Lifestyle modification — patient endorses dietary changes; no formal structured program documented

Cigna has denied semaglutide (Wegovy) as non-formulary. It is my clinical opinion that the formulary alternatives have not been demonstrated to be appropriate or tolerable for this patient given her history. Appeal is being submitted on clinical necessity grounds.
