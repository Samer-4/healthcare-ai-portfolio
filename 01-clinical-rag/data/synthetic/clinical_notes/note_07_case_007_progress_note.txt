Mr. Morrison is a 55-year-old male with a documented history of chronic obesity (BMI 31.2 kg/m²) presenting for ongoing management of weight-related illness and cardiovascular risk optimization. Adherence was documented as good throughout the program duration.

- **Structured Exercise Program (March 2022 – December 2022, 9 months):** Patient engaged in a formal exercise regimen consisting of 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity aerobic activity, as recommended per AHA/ACC guidelines. He reported consistent participation; however, weight loss was minimal (approximately 4 lbs total), and long-term maintenance was unsuccessful. He cites progressive fatigue and work schedule demands as barriers to sustaining this regimen independently.

- **Behavioral / Lifestyle Modification Counseling (June 2022 – February 2023, 8 months):** Patient was referred to and completed a structured behavioral weight loss program including individual nutritional counseling (registered dietitian, biweekly visits), motivational interviewing, and behavioral modification strategies. Documentation from the program is available upon request. Net weight change at program conclusion: –5 lbs with subsequent regain of 7 lbs over the following 4 months.

He was counseled on concurrent low-fat dietary adherence. Weight loss achieved was approximately 3 lbs over 6 months, falling well below the clinically meaningful threshold of ≥5% body weight reduction. Therapy was discontinued in October 2023 due to intolerable side effects and inadequate efficacy.

- **Pharmacologic Therapy – Phentermine/topiramate ER (Qsymia) 3.75 mg/23 mg titrated to 7.5 mg/46 mg daily (December 2023 – May 2024, 6 months):** Patient was initiated on phentermine/topiramate ER at the lowest dose with planned titration. Despite titration per protocol, total weight loss at 6 months was 5 lbs (approximately 2.3% of body weight), below the ≥5% response threshold used to assess clinical benefit. Given the adverse effect profile and inadequate response, therapy was discontinued in May 2024.
