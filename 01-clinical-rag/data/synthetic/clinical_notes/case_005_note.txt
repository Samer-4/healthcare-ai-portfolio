Patel is a 41-year-old female with established diagnoses of polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS; E28.2) and class II obesity (E66.01) presenting today for follow-up regarding ongoing weight management and hormonal dysregulation. Per Humana's denial, orlistat is identified as the covered alternative. Ms. Patel was prescribed orlistat 120 mg three times daily with meals beginning **March 2023**, following a documented discussion of formulary-preferred options.
