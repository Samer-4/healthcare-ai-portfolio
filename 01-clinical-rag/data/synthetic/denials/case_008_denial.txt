## BASIS FOR DENIAL

This request has been denied based on **insufficient clinical documentation** to establish that all required criteria under Aetna's **Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580 — Obesity: Drug Therapy** (effective January 1, 2025) have been met. Tirzepatide (Zepbound) is a GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist approved by the FDA for chronic weight management in adults with a body mass index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m², or ≥ 27 kg/m² with at least one weight-related comorbidity. While the member's reported BMI of 39 kg/m² and documented diagnoses of polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS) and insulin resistance may satisfy the threshold eligibility criteria, the submitted documentation does not sufficiently address all of the following required elements as outlined in Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580, Section III.B:

**1. Documented Evidence of Prior Step Therapy**
The submitted clinical record does not include adequate documentation demonstrating that the member has participated in a structured, supervised behavioral weight management program for a minimum of **six (6) consecutive months** within the past two (2) years. Specifically, the submission lacks:
- Provider-signed attestation or records from a qualifying weight loss program (e.g., physician-supervised diet program, hospital-based behavioral program, or equivalent commercial program with documented medical oversight);
- Records of program attendance, frequency of participation, and documented weight outcomes during participation.

**2. Inadequate Documentation of Prior Pharmacological Therapy**
Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580 requires documentation that the member has had an **adequate trial and documented failure, intolerance, or contraindication** to at least one (1) formulary-preferred weight management agent (e.g., orlistat, phentermine/topiramate ER [Qsymia], naltrexone/bupropion ER [Contrave]) prior to the initiation of a GLP-1/GIP agonist.
