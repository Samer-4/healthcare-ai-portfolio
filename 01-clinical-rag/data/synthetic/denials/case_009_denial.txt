---

**BASIS FOR DENIAL**

Humana's Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580 establishes the following criteria that **must all be met** for approval of liraglutide 3.0 mg (Saxenda®) for chronic weight management:

> **1.** The member has a Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m², **or** a BMI ≥ 27 kg/m² in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbidity (e.g., type 2 diabetes mellitus, hypertension, dyslipidemia, obstructive sleep apnea); **AND**
>
> **2.** Documentation of a **minimum of six (6) consecutive months** of participation in a structured, clinically supervised lifestyle intervention program encompassing reduced-calorie diet, increased physical activity, and behavioral modification, with documented and measurable outcomes, attempted **prior** to initiation of pharmacological therapy; **AND**
>
> **3.** Documentation that prior FDA-approved pharmacological agents for chronic weight management (e.g., orlistat, phentermine/topiramate extended-release, naltrexone/bupropion extended-release) have been trialed at adequate doses and have either failed to achieve clinically meaningful weight loss (defined as ≥ 5% reduction in total body weight) or were discontinued due to documented intolerability; **AND**
>
> **4.** The prescribing physician has documented that the member is an appropriate candidate for liraglutide therapy, including the absence of contraindications (e.g., personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2, active or prior pancreatitis).
