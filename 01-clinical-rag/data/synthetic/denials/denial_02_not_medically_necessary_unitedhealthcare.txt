### REASON FOR DENIAL

This request has been denied based on the following finding:

The submitted clinical documentation does not meet the criteria outlined in **UnitedHealthcare Clinical Policy Bulletin No. CPB-OBE-0580.20, "Anti-Obesity Medications and Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonists and GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Chronic Weight Management"** (Effective Date: January 1, 2025; reviewed March 15, 2025).

---

### CLINICAL RATIONALE

UnitedHealthcare's Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-OBE-0580.20 establishes the following medically necessary coverage criteria for tirzepatide (Zepbound) for chronic weight management in adult members. A request must satisfy **all applicable** criteria for approval. Based on the clinical documentation submitted, the following criterion (or criteria) was **not met**:

**1. BMI Threshold with Qualifying Comorbidity – Partially Met / Insufficient Documentation**

The policy requires one of the following:

- **(a)** Body Mass Index (BMI) of **≥ 30 kg/m²** with **at least one weight-related comorbidity** (e.g., type 2 diabetes, obstructive sleep apnea, cardiovascular disease, hyperlipidemia, or hypertension), **AND** documented evidence that the comorbidity is directly attributed to or meaningfully worsened by excess weight; **OR**
- **(b)** Body Mass Index (BMI) of **≥ 35 kg/m²** independent of comorbid conditions.

The submitted records indicate a BMI of **35 kg/m²** and a diagnosis of **hypertension**. While the member's BMI meets the numeric threshold under criterion (b) above, the submitted documentation reflects a BMI measurement taken on **November 18, 2024**, which exceeds UnitedHealthcare's requirement that BMI documentation be obtained **within 90 days of the authorization request date** of June 5, 2025. The submitted BMI therefore cannot be accepted as current and valid per policy requirements.
