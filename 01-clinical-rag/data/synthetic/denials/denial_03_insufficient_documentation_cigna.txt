### REASON FOR DENIAL: Insufficient Documentation to Support Medical Necessity

Cigna's determination is based on a review of the clinical documentation submitted with the prior authorization request and the applicable Cigna Clinical Policy Bulletin. The submitted documentation does not meet the required criteria for approval of semaglutide (Ozempic®) for the following reasons:

**1. Applicable Policy:**
This determination was made in accordance with **Cigna Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580 – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonists**, effective January 1, 2025, and the member's applicable Certificate of Coverage.

**2. Coverage Criteria Not Met – Established Type 2 Diabetes Diagnosis:**
Ozempic® (semaglutide injectable) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist indicated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the treatment of adults with **established Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)** as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control. Per Cigna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580, coverage of Ozempic® requires documented confirmation of a T2DM diagnosis meeting diagnostic threshold criteria (i.e., fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL on two separate occasions, HbA1c ≥ 6.5%, or 2-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during an oral glucose tolerance test, consistent with ADA Standards of Medical Care).

The submitted documentation reflects a diagnosis of **prediabetes** (HbA1c range consistent with 5.7%–6.4%), which does not constitute a confirmed diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus as required under the applicable policy.
