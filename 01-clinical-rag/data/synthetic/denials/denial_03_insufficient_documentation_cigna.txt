---

### BASIS FOR DENIAL

Semaglutide (Ozempic®) is indicated and covered by Cigna Healthcare for members diagnosed with **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus** who meet all applicable clinical criteria. Coverage requires documentation of the following, as outlined in CPB-0580.4:

1. **Confirmed diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)**, defined by HbA1c ≥ 6.5%, fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL on two separate occasions, or a two-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during an oral glucose tolerance test (per ADA diagnostic standards); **AND**

2. **Documentation of HbA1c level** at or above the member's individualized treatment threshold as established by the treating provider; **AND**

3. **Evidence of an adequate trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance** to at least one first-line oral antidiabetic agent (specifically metformin, unless contraindicated or not tolerated), defined as a minimum 90-day trial at a therapeutic dose with documented failure to achieve glycemic targets; **AND**

4. **Current HbA1c value** obtained within the past 90 days submitted with the authorization request.
