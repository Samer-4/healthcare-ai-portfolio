### BASIS FOR DENIAL

Coverage of tirzepatide (Zepbound™) under **Aetna CPB 0580** requires that **all** of the following criteria be documented in the prior authorization request. The following required elements were **absent or incomplete** in the submitted request:

---

**1. Documentation of a Qualifying Structured Weight Management Program (Required — Not Submitted)**

CPB 0580, Section IV.A.2 requires documentation demonstrating that the member has participated in a **supervised, comprehensive behavioral weight management program** for a minimum of **six (6) consecutive months** within the preceding 24 months. This program must include all three of the following components:

- (a) Reduced-calorie dietary intervention supervised by a qualified provider or registered dietitian;
- (b) Physical activity counseling and structured exercise programming;
- (c) Behavioral modification counseling conducted by a licensed behavioral health provider.

The submitted prior authorization did not include records, visit notes, or a program summary documenting participation in or completion of such a program. A provider attestation alone is **not sufficient** to satisfy this requirement. Supporting documentation must include dated visit records or a written program summary signed by the supervising provider(s).

---

**2. Documentation of Stable, Optimized First-Line Pharmacotherapy Trial (Required — Insufficient)**

CPB 0580, Section IV.A.3 requires documentation that the member has trialed and demonstrated an inadequate response to at least **one (1) FDA-approved weight management pharmacotherapy** at maximally tolerated dosing for a minimum of **12 weeks**, unless contraindicated.
