## CLINICAL RATIONALE

The submitted clinical documentation was reviewed by a UnitedHealthcare Medical Director, who is a licensed physician qualified in the applicable specialty area. Based on this review, authorization for tirzepatide (Zepbound) is denied for the following reasons:

**1. Failure to Meet Step Therapy Requirements**

Per Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580.19, Section III.B, coverage of tirzepatide (Zepbound) for weight management in members with a concurrent diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus requires documented trial and therapeutic failure, contraindication, or intolerance of **at least one (1) formulary-preferred GLP-1 receptor agonist agent**, specifically:

- **Semaglutide injection (Ozempic)** — for concurrent T2DM indication; OR
- **Semaglutide injection (Wegovy)** — for weight management indication

The clinical documentation submitted as of the date of this determination does **not** contain evidence of a prior adequate trial (defined as a minimum of **90 consecutive days** at a therapeutic maintenance dose) of a preferred formulary GLP-1 agent, nor documentation of a clinically significant adverse reaction, contraindication, or other clinical reason precluding use of a covered preferred alternative.
