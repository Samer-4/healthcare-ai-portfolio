### BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Clinical Policy Bulletin Reference:** Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin (CPB) **HUM-PHARM-0742**: *Anti-Obesity Pharmacotherapy: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Adjunctive Weight Management Agents* (Effective Date: July 1, 2024; Last Reviewed: October 15, 2024)

**Denial Reason:** Not Medically Necessary as Requested — **Preferred Alternative Agent Available and Not Adequately Trialed**

### CLINICAL RATIONALE

Humana's review of the submitted clinical documentation indicates that the request for **Wegovy® (semaglutide 2.4 mg subcutaneous injection)** does not meet the criteria outlined in **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin HUM-PHARM-0742**, specifically **Section IV.B.2**, which requires documented trial and failure of, or documented clinical contraindication to, **at least one Tier 1 preferred formulary agent** prior to authorization of a Tier 2 non-preferred anti-obesity medication.

Based on the information provided, the following determination was made:

1. **Preferred Formulary Alternative Available:** The member's current **Humana Choice PPO** formulary includes **orlistat 120 mg (Xenical®/generic)** and **phentermine/topiramate extended-release (Qsymia®)** as Tier 1 preferred agents indicated for chronic weight management in adults with an initial BMI ≥30 kg/m² or ≥27 kg/m² with at least one weight-related comorbidity. The member's documented BMI of **37 kg/m²** and diagnosis of **polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS)** qualify her for these alternatives.

2. **Absence of Documented Adequate Trial:** The clinical documentation submitted does not reflect an adequate trial (defined under CPB HUM-PHARM-0742, Section IV.B.2 as **a minimum of 12 consecutive weeks at the maximally tolerated therapeutic dose**) of any Tier 1 preferred anti-obesity agent. Failure is defined as less than 5% total body weight loss at 12 weeks of consistent use at a therapeutic dose.
