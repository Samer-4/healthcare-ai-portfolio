### BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Denial Reason: Alternative Covered Therapy Available (Step Therapy Requirement Not Met)**

This request has been reviewed in accordance with **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin No. CPB-0580: Anti-Obesity Agents and GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Chronic Weight Management (Revised: February 1, 2025)**, as well as applicable state and federal coverage guidelines and evidence-based clinical criteria.

Based on the clinical information submitted and available in the member's records, Humana is unable to authorize Wegovy® (semaglutide 2.4 mg) at this time. The denial is based on the following:

**1. Step Therapy Requirement Has Not Been Satisfied**

Humana's Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580 requires that members requesting coverage for Wegovy® (semaglutide 2.4 mg injection) for the indication of chronic weight management (obesity, BMI ≥ 30 kg/m² or ≥ 27 kg/m² with at least one weight-related comorbidity) must first demonstrate an adequate trial and documented therapeutic failure, intolerance, or contraindication to **at least one (1) Humana-preferred formulary-covered anti-obesity pharmacotherapy**, which currently includes:

- **Orlistat 120 mg** (generic; Tier 2, covered under applicable pharmacy benefit)
- **Phentermine/topiramate ER** (Qsymia®; Tier 3, covered with prior authorization)
- **Bupropion/naltrexone ER** (Contrave®; Tier 3, covered with prior authorization)

A review of the documentation submitted with the prior authorization request, as well as the member's available pharmacy and medical claims history, does not reflect a prior trial of any of the above-referenced preferred covered alternatives for a clinically adequate duration (defined under CPB-0580 as a minimum of **twelve (12) consecutive weeks** at the maximum
