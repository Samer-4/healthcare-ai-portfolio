## CLINICAL BASIS FOR DENIAL

UnitedHealthcare's Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580 requires that **all** of the following criteria be satisfied for coverage of tirzepatide (Zepbound) for chronic weight management:

**1. Body Mass Index (BMI) Threshold — Criterion Met**
The member's documented BMI of **35 kg/m²** meets the minimum threshold requirement (BMI ≥ 30, or ≥ 27 with at least one qualifying weight-related comorbidity).

**2. Documented Comorbidity — Criterion Met**
The member has a documented diagnosis of **hypertension**, which constitutes a qualifying weight-related comorbidity under CPB 0580, Section III.B.2.

**3. Prior Documented Participation in a Structured, Supervised Behavioral Modification Program — Criterion NOT Met**
CPB 0580, Section III.B.4 requires documentation of **at least six (6) consecutive months** of participation in a **comprehensive, medically supervised weight management program** within the preceding **24 months**, inclusive of all of the following components:

- Reduced-calorie dietary intervention supervised or prescribed by a licensed healthcare professional
- Structured physical activity program with documented participation
- Behavioral counseling provided by a qualified behavioral health provider or registered dietitian

The clinical documentation submitted in support of this request does not demonstrate participation in, or completion of, a qualifying supervised weight management program meeting the above criteria. The submitted records reference a general recommendation for diet and exercise by the treating physician but do not include documentation of formal enrollment, attendance records, or outcomes data from a qualifying structured program.
