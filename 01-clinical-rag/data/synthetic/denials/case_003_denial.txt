After careful clinical review of the submitted documentation, **this request does not meet the criteria for approval** as outlined in Cigna's **Medical Coverage Policy: Clinical Policy Bulletin (CPB) 0580 — Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonists and Dual GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonists**, effective January 1, 2025.

### BASIS FOR DENIAL

The request for semaglutide (Ozempic®) has been denied for the following reasons:

**1. Indication Not Established for Submitted Diagnosis**

Semaglutide (Ozempic®) is FDA-approved for the improvement of glycemic control in adults with **Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM)**. The clinical documentation submitted for Ms. Maria T. Reyes (MRN-100236) reflects a current diagnosis of **prediabetes (ICD-10: R73.09)** and **obesity (BMI 33)**. The submitted diagnosis code of E11.65 (Type 2 diabetes mellitus) is **not substantiated** by the clinical records provided. Specifically, no documentation was received confirming a formal diagnosis of Type 2 diabetes mellitus as defined by current American Diabetes Association (ADA) standards, including any of the following:

- Fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL on two separate occasions;
- Two-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during an oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT);
- HbA1c ≥ 6.5% on two separate occasions; or
- Random plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL in a patient with classic symptoms of hyperglycemia.

The single most recent HbA1c value on file (submitted as 6.1%) is consistent with **prediabetes**, not Type 2 diabetes mellitus, and does not support approval of Ozempic® under CPB 0580.

**2. Step Therapy Requirements Not Met**

Per **CPB 0580, Section IV.B**, coverage of semaglutide (Ozempic®) for Type 2 diabetes mellitus requires documentation that the member has had an **inadequate clinical response, contraindication, or intolerance** to at least **one first-line oral antidiabetic agent** (e.g., metformin). The submitted documentation does not include:

- Evidence of a trial of metformin or other first-line oral antidiabetic therapy of adequate dose and duration (minimum 90 days);
- Documentation of intolerance, adverse effects, or contraindication precluding use of first-line agents; or
- Laboratory values
