### BASIS FOR DENIAL: OFF-LABEL USE / COVERAGE CRITERIA NOT MET

**Cigna's determination is that the requested use of semaglutide (Ozempic®) for the treatment of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) in an overweight patient without a diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus constitutes an off-label use that does not meet the criteria for medical necessity under Cigna Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB 0580.**

The clinical basis for this denial is as follows:

**1. FDA-Approved Indications Not Met**
Semaglutide (Ozempic®) is approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) solely for the following indications:
- Adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)**
- Reduction of the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events in adults with T2DM and established cardiovascular disease

The submitted documentation does not reflect a diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus for Mr. Thompson. Accordingly, the primary FDA-approved indication for Ozempic® is not satisfied.

**2. Overweight Status Does Not Meet Weight Management Threshold**
Cigna's CPB 0580 further recognizes coverage for GLP-1 receptor agonists indicated for chronic weight management (e.g., semaglutide 2.4 mg [Wegovy®]) in patients meeting **both** of the following criteria:
- Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m², **OR** BMI ≥ 27 kg/m² with at least one weight-related comorbidity (e.g., hypertension, dyslipidemia, obstructive sleep apnea, or T2DM)
- Enrollment in or referral to a concurrent structured diet and behavioral modification program
