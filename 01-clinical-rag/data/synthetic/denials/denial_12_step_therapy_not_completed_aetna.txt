### REASON FOR DENIAL

**Step Therapy Requirements Not Met — Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580 (Obesity Management — Anti-Obesity Pharmacotherapy), effective January 1, 2025**

Tirzepatide (Zepbound) is classified under Aetna's **Tier 3 specialty anti-obesity agent** formulary category. Under **Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin (CPB) 0580, Section IV.B.2**, coverage for Tier 3 anti-obesity agents requires documented evidence that the member has completed an adequate trial of **at least one (1) Tier 2 formulary anti-obesity agent** prior to requesting authorization of a Tier 3 agent, unless a specific contraindication or intolerance is documented and supported by clinical records.

**Required Step Therapy Sequence (CPB 0580, Section IV.B.2):**

> *Step 1 — Lifestyle Intervention:* Documentation of enrollment in or referral to a structured, supervised weight management program (e.g., physician-supervised caloric restriction, behavioral counseling, or an accredited program such as the Diabetes Prevention Program or equivalent) for a minimum of **three (3) consecutive months**, with documented outcomes.

> *Step 2 — Tier 2 Pharmacotherapy Trial:* A minimum **three (3) month adequate trial** of at least one (1) preferred, formulary Tier 2 anti-obesity agent, which includes:
> - Orlistat (Xenical/Alli)
> - Phentermine/topiramate extended-release (Qsymia) — if clinically appropriate
> - Naltrexone/bupropion extended-release (Contrave)
> - Liraglutide 3.0 mg (Saxenda) *(if BMI criteria met and no contraindication)*

> Step 2 trial must include documentation of: medication name, dose titration history, duration of use, weight outcomes, and reason for discontinuation or therapeutic failure, if applicable.
