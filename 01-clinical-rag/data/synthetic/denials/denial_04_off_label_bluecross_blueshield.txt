**REASON FOR DENIAL**

**Primary Denial Reason:** The requested medication does not meet the medical necessity and coverage criteria established under **BCBSIL Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580: Weight Management Pharmacotherapy and Anti-Obesity Agents (Revised March 1, 2025)**.

**Clinical Rationale:**

Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580 specifies that coverage for liraglutide 3 mg (Saxenda®), as indicated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for chronic weight management, requires that the member meet **all** of the following criteria:

1. The member has a Body Mass Index (BMI) of **≥ 30 kg/m²**, **OR** a BMI of **≥ 27 kg/m²** in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbid condition (e.g., type 2 diabetes mellitus, hypertension, or dyslipidemia); **AND**

2. The member has been enrolled in or is actively participating in a **structured, comprehensive behavioral modification program**, including dietary counseling and increased physical activity, for a minimum of **six (6) months**, with documented outcomes; **AND**

3. The member does not have a **diagnosis of type 1 or type 2 diabetes mellitus** for which liraglutide at a lower dose (Victoza®) would represent the appropriate, on-label treatment; **AND**

4. **Prior pharmacotherapy failure** has been documented: the member must have trialed and failed at least one (1) other FDA-approved weight management pharmacotherapy (e.g., phentermine/topiramate ER [Qsymia®], naltrexone/bupropion [Contrave®], or orlistat), with documentation of the agent used, duration, and reason for discontinuation or inadequate response.

**Based on the clinical documentation submitted, the following criteria were not met:**

- The submitted records document a current BMI of **30 kg/m²**. While this meets the minimum BMI threshold on its face, the clinical documentation submitted does not confirm that the member's weight-related comorbid condition — obstructive sleep apnea — has been formally evaluated in relation to weight management as required under Policy Bulletin 0580, Section IV(B)(2).

- There is **no documentation of participation in a structured behavioral modification program**
