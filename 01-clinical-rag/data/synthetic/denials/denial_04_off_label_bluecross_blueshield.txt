### CLINICAL RATIONALE

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has approved liraglutide 3.0 mg (Saxenda®) as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity for chronic weight management in adults meeting **one of the following criteria:**

1. An initial Body Mass Index (BMI) of **≥ 30 kg/m²** (obesity); **or**
2. An initial BMI of **≥ 27 kg/m²** in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbidity, such as hypertension, type 2 diabetes mellitus, or dyslipidemia.

**Per documentation submitted, the member's current BMI is recorded at 30.0 kg/m².** Based on a clinical review of the submitted records, the following deficiencies were identified that preclude approval under Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580:

1. **BMI Threshold — Borderline/Unverified:** The member's BMI of 30.0 kg/m² meets the numeric threshold for obesity as defined by FDA labeling. However, per BCBSIL Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580, Section IV.A.2, approval at a BMI of 30.0–30.9 kg/m² requires submission of a **documented, recent weight measurement (within 60 days) obtained in a clinical setting** with height and weight recorded in the provider's office notes. The submitted authorization request did not include supporting clinical documentation confirming this measurement. A BMI stated solely on the authorization form without accompanying clinical records is insufficient for approval
