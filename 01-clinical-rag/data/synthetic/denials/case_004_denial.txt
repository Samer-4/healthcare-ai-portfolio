## BASIS FOR DENIAL

### 1. Policy Citation

This determination is made pursuant to **BlueCross BlueShield Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580 — GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Obesity Pharmacotherapy**, effective January 1, 2025, as well as the member's applicable **Pharmacy Benefit Management Coverage Guidelines, Section 4.7: Anti-Obesity Agents**. All criteria cited herein are consistent with the member's current Certificate of Coverage and Summary Plan Description.

---

### 2. Clinical Rationale

BlueCross BlueShield's review of the submitted clinical documentation reveals the following:

**(a) Prescriber Specialty — Outside Authorized Scope of Practice for This Indication**

Saxenda® (liraglutide 3.0 mg/mL) is FDA-approved as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity for chronic weight management. Per **Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580, Section III.B.2**, prior authorization requests for anti-obesity agents, including GLP-1 receptor agonists indicated for chronic weight management, must be initiated by or in active consultation with a provider in one of the following specialties: **Endocrinology, Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Gastroenterology, or a board-certified Obesity Medicine specialist**. The requesting provider, **Dr. Lauren Mackey, MD**, is credentialed within this plan in the specialty of **Sleep Medicine**. The submitted documentation does not reflect a co-managing provider from a qualifying specialty, nor does it include documentation that Dr. Mackey holds Obesity Medicine board certification (ABOM or ASBP). Accordingly, the prescribing arrangement does not satisfy the specialty requirement established under applicable policy.
