### POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

This determination is based on **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin No. CPB-0580: Pharmacologic Management of Obesity and Weight Management Agents (Effective Date: January 1, 2025; Reviewed: April 14, 2025)**, as well as applicable formulary management guidelines under the member's current benefit plan.

**Reason for Denial: Alternative Covered Drug Available**

The submitted request for semaglutide 2.4 mg subcutaneous (Wegovy®) does not satisfy step therapy requirements under CPB-0580, Section IV.B, which requires documented trial and inadequate response, intolerance, or contraindication to **at least one (1) formulary-preferred weight management agent** prior to approval of a non-preferred branded GLP-1 receptor agonist indicated solely for chronic weight management.

Based on the clinical information received, the following criteria were evaluated:

1. **Member Eligibility for Weight Management Pharmacotherapy:** Met. Member Ashley R. Patel presents with a documented BMI of 37 kg/m² and a qualifying weight-related comorbidity (Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome, E28.2), meeting the threshold criteria under CPB-0580, Section III.A.2 for consideration of pharmacologic obesity treatment.

2. **Step Therapy Requirement – Formulary-Preferred Alternative:** **Not Met.** The submitted documentation does not demonstrate a prior adequate trial (minimum 12 consecutive weeks at therapeutic dose) with a Humana formulary-preferred covered alternative. Under the member's current plan benefit, the following agents are covered at a lower formulary tier and are considered clinically appropriate first-line options for weight management in eligible members:

   - **Orlistat (Xenical®/generic)** – Tier 2
   - **Phentermine/topiramate extended-release (Qsymia®)** – Tier 3 (PA required; clinical criteria less restrictive)
   - **Naltrexone/bupropion extended-release (Contrave®)** – Tier 3

   The submitted clinical documentation references prior dietary counseling and lifestyle intervention but does not include documentation of a trial with any of the above-listed formulary
