**BASIS FOR DENIAL**

This determination was made in accordance with:

- **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin No. CPB-0580: Pharmacological Treatment of Obesity and Weight Management**
- **Humana Pharmacy Coverage and Reimbursement Policy PRx-2024-0031**
- **Evidence-based clinical guidelines as established by the American Association of Clinical Endocrinology (AACE) and the Obesity Medicine Association (OMA)**

Liraglutide 3 mg (Saxenda®) is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist indicated as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity for chronic weight management. Per Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580, coverage of liraglutide (Saxenda®) requires that **all** of the following criteria be met:

1. **The member has a Body Mass Index (BMI) of ≥ 30 kg/m², OR a BMI of ≥ 27 kg/m² with at least one weight-related comorbidity** (e.g., type 2 diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidemia, obstructive sleep apnea); **AND**
2. **Documentation of a supervised, structured weight-loss program** lasting a minimum of **six (6) months** within the 24 months prior to the request, including dietary counseling, behavioral modification, and documented participation in a physical activity regimen, with records demonstrating clinically insufficient weight loss response; **AND**
3. **Documentation that the member has attempted and failed, or has a contraindication to, at least one other approved pharmacological weight-management agent** (e.g., phentermine-topiramate [Qsymia®], naltrexone-bupropion [Contrave®], or orlistat [Xenical®/Alli®]); **AND**
4. **The prescribing physician is, or has coordinated care with, a specialist in obesity medicine, endocrinology, or a formally structured multidisciplinary weight management program.**
