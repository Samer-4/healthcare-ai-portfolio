### REASON FOR DENIAL: OFF-LABEL USE / DOES NOT MEET CLINICAL COVERAGE CRITERIA

This request has been denied based on the following findings:

**1. FDA-Approved Indication Not Met**

Semaglutide injection (Ozempic®) holds U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval for the following indications only:

- As an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)**
- To reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in adults with **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and established cardiovascular disease**

The diagnosis submitted for this authorization request — **Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD), ICD-10: K76.0** — does not correspond to an FDA-approved indication for semaglutide (Ozempic®). The use of Ozempic® for the treatment of NAFLD/Non-Alcoholic Steatohepatitis (NASH) constitutes an **off-label use** of this medication.

**Please note:** Wegovy® (semaglutide 2.4 mg, the higher-dose formulation approved for chronic weight management) is a distinct FDA-approved product with its own separate coverage criteria. The request as submitted is specifically for **Ozempic®**, which is not approved for weight management as a primary indication, and the member does not carry a diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in the submitted clinical documentation.
