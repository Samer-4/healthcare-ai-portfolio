### BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Reason for Denial: Step Therapy Requirements Not Completed**

This denial is issued pursuant to **Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580: Obesity/Overweight Management Medications**, effective date July 1, 2024, as well as applicable plan benefit documents governing the member's coverage under Group Plan No. 00774412-01.

Aetna's Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580 requires that members satisfy a documented step therapy protocol prior to authorization of GLP-1 receptor agonist medications indicated for chronic weight management, including semaglutide (Wegovy®). Specifically, the policy requires **all of the following criteria** to be met:

**1. Documented Failure of Prior Pharmacological Therapy**

The submitted clinical documentation does not demonstrate an adequate trial and documented clinical failure of **at least one (1) FDA-approved first-line weight management medication** from the following approved step therapy agents:

- Orlistat (Xenical®/Alli®) — minimum 90-day trial
- Phentermine/topiramate extended-release (Qsymia®) — minimum 90-day trial
- Naltrexone/bupropion extended-release (Contrave®) — minimum 90-day trial

The records submitted on **January 9, 2025** reflect no claims history or documented prescribing of any of the above agents for this member within the applicable lookback period. Documentation of a contraindication, intolerance, or clinical reason for bypassing each of the above agents was not included with the prior authorization request.

**2. Documented Structured Behavioral and Lifestyle Intervention Program**

The submitted documentation does not include evidence of the member's participation in a **structured, supervised behavioral weight management program** (defined under CPB 0580, Section IV.B as a minimum 90-day medically supervised program involving dietary counseling, physical activity guidance, and behavioral modification components). Aetna requires documentation that such a program was attempted prior to initiation of pharmacological therapy at the level of GLP-1 receptor agonists.

**3. Prescriber Attestation**

A complete prescriber attestation form (Form UM-PA-580-OB) confirming member's weight history, comorbid conditions, prior therapy attempts, and clinical justification for the requested agent over step therapy alternatives was not included in the submitted request.
